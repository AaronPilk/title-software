# Filing company and title documents

The upload flow separates choosing a destination from reviewing the individual files. Company setup stays focused on business details and documents; formation and approval reviews remain available when staff need them.

## Choose a destination

Start in a company's Documents tab to keep uploads attached to that company. Starting in the main Documents vault requires a company selection unless its company filter already provides one. General uploads never silently choose the first company in the portfolio.

Company documents cover the business itself: formation records, applications, agreements, disclosures and logos. Choose Logo / branding for a logo. Title-file documents belong to a particular property's existing file, such as searches, deeds and opinions. Only title files within the selected company are available.

## Add and review files

Choose the original files, then review the filename, destination, category and access for each. A batch may contain different categories. Applications require Restricted access. Review replacement versions before saving. Users can go back or remove a selected file before anything is uploaded.

The original file bytes are preserved. Saving documents does not publish, email, approve the contents or mark extracted fields reviewed. Prepared commitments, final policies, CPLs and corrections keep their dedicated workflow upload controls and preparation references.

## Find what was saved

The main vault has Company documents and Title-file documents filters. Filed under shows whether an original belongs to the company or a property file. Company and access filters can narrow either group.

Inside a company, Documents shows its company records first. The package reader uses that same set. Property-specific originals appear under the expandable Title-file documents section with their title-file number and address; their package review is in the title file's Final sources. Requests and approvals remains under Documents for logos, templates and other requested company materials.

Existing-company application and approval reviews are expandable in Onboarding. Formation and approval history is expandable in the company's Overview. These presentation changes do not mark missing evidence complete or change business authority.
