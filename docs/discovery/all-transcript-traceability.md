## Priority and scope reconciliation

Stephenie prioritizes her onboarding and shared vault, then additional access and integrations (S 16:44–17:01; 25:54–27:37). Tyler prioritizes finals and simple revisions over autonomous initial commitments (T 15:48–16:46; 48:27–49:55; 63:52–66:03). His clearest measurable pain is almost 80 pending finals, requiring about 5–10 focused minutes each (T 64:06–64:49; 67:35–67:59). At exactly 80, that is approximately 6h40–13h20 of work before interruption costs, not verified achievable savings.

The expanded local implementation remains distinct from the other speaker’s unvalidated AI promises. Preserve the surrounding company/finance/partner workspace while completing connected local workflows. Model real external outcomes separately from locally prepared artifacts.

Highest-impact work that is possible without backend/API access:

1. **Complete inbox intake:** create/import local sample messages, classify new commitment/final/revision/company work, match company before file, ingest real local attachments, create an order when unmatched, preserve the conversation reference and deduplicate processing.
2. **Initial commitment preparation:** PTO/source review, property and contacts, prior-policy/tacking review, requirements/exceptions, policy products and endorsements, CPL review and a versioned preparation packet plus reply draft. Do not fabricate official approved forms.
3. **Separate order and policy products:** owner/loan products, liability, premiums, issuance and delivery tracked separately; aggregate reports must not double-count the old order premium.
4. **Evidence-backed onboarding:** application and welcome records, formation/EIN records, per-underwriter approvals, materials and reviewer handoffs rather than seven independent completion booleans.
5. **Close snapshots:** dated source policy rows, editable illustrative terms and adjustments, frozen company/member allocations, reviewed remittance/statement references, revision history and approved partner publication.
6. **Partner records:** company/member access preview, approved documents/statements, independently dated received/closed/rejected/recovered outcomes and follow-up progress.

## Cross-module acceptance gates for the expanded local build

1. **One connected new-order story:** enter a new attorney email and real sample attachments → confirm company → create order → manually review PTO/parties/property/prior-policy references → configure products/endorsements/CPL review → prepare a labeled packet and reply draft. No seeded IDs required.
2. **One connected final story:** ingest a later FTO email onto that same order → classify combined/separate instruments → reconcile dated/recording/loan details → document commitment clearance → prepare product-specific final handoff → record only explicit local simulation or manual provider evidence → build recipient-specific delivery package.
3. **One revision story:** new message → correct JV/file → bounded before/after change → stale-state checks → local apply → obtain/upload current commitment → review draft. Repeated processing is idempotent; unsupported changes remain held.
4. **One company story:** create prospect → record welcome/application → attach formation and EIN evidence → hand off to John → record multiple underwriter approvals → review company materials → publish selected versions. Missing/changed evidence affects readiness; sensitive references never leak to partner preview.
5. **One accounting/partner story:** select source policy rows by explicit period → reconcile illustrative terms and adjustments → freeze close → freeze member allocations → publish one company statement → modify current inputs. The old close and statement remain reproducible, and the new draft is distinct.
6. **Outcome definitions:** received, closed, issued, delivered, rejected, recovered and remitted are independent events. Product counts and order counts are labeled and cannot inflate each other.
7. **Migration:** preserve existing company/order/document IDs, uploaded assets, original source/current/proposed text, revisions and notes. Legacy order money becomes one legacy product or an explicitly unmapped amount; do not invent owner/loan amounts or historical approvals. Existing review evidence is reopened only where new dependencies make it insufficient, with the reason visible.
8. **No fabricated external success:** database rows and local exports do not mean SoftPro write, jacket issuance, CPL generation, government filing, Missive send, bank action, legal approval or real multi-user security. Each requires actual supported integration and evidence later.
